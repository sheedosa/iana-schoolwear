# I&A Schoolwear storefront — guidance for Claude Code

Static React storefront (Vite + Tailwind) deployed to GitHub Pages; Shopify is the commerce backend via the Storefront API.
Version 4: **the shop is an order form.** A parent has a list and wants it done. Every page is rows, sizes and one button.

## Rules
1. **Facts come from `src/data/site.ts` and `src/data/catalogue.json`.** Never invent policies, prices, hours, delivery charges, stock levels or claims. Anything not verified on iafashions.co.uk goes in `SITE.toConfirm`, not on the page. There is no peak-season notice in `SITE`: do not write one.
2. **Commerce goes through `src/lib/shopify.ts` and `src/lib/cart.tsx` only.** Demo mode (catalogue JSON) and Shopify mode share one interface. Do not call Shopify from components. Adding several sizes calls the basket's own `add(handle, size, qty)` once per size, in order (`OrderTable`).
3. **Every page sets `<Seo>`** (title, description, canonical, OG image, JSON-LD). `npm run build` prerenders all routes; check `dist/<route>/index.html` after adding a page and add it to `scripts/prerender.mjs`.
4. **Pictures are the shop's own product photos, shown as cut-outs** (64px in a row, 320px on the product page). No lifestyle photographs, no hero image, no banners, no generated pictures, no children's faces. A product with no photo gets a small garment outline in its row and no picture column on its page. **Which photos show, and which leads, is `src/lib/photo-rules.ts`** (the catalogue is protected, so this is the display layer): `HIDDEN` lists supplier shots that show a child's face (they are never shown, and their files are gone from `public/img/products`), `CLOSE_UPS` lists crest and detail close-ups so a product leads with its whole garment. Pick a picture with `photos(p)` / `firstPhoto(p)`, never `p.images`: the row picture, the product page and gallery, a school's mark, the Product JSON-LD and the share cards all go through it. A new photo with a face goes in `HIDDEN` (or is not added); then re-run the cards. The share image is built by `/home/claude/builds/v5/iana-schoolwear/_scripts/og.cjs` from the lockup and type (Fredoka heading, Lexend line).
5. **Look:**
   - Logo: the official I & A Schoolwear lockups in `public/img/logo/brand/` (white on the blue bar). They do not change. Cormorant exists only inside the lockup.
   - Colours (tokens in `index.css`): White ground; School Blue `#4169e1` for the bar, field borders, table rules and focus; `#2f55c8` for link text and pressed keys; Blue tint `#edf1fd` for hover, the chosen school and a size line with a quantity; line `#cfd6ea`; Ochre `#45353a` text, `#665359` secondary text. School Yellow `#ffde59` is the add-to-basket button and the basket count, nothing else. No Sage, no brown bands, no dark footer.
   - Type: two faces, changed only through `--font-display` and `--font-sans` in `index.css`. Schoolwear owns the rounded letter in the I&A group: no other face may be added and no heading may fall back to Lexend.
     - **Fredoka** (rounded) 600 for h1, h2, h3, a school's name wherever schools are listed (`.name`: the table at 600, the search list at 500) and the initials in a school's mark; Fredoka 500 for buttons (`.btn`, `.bar-btn`) and the phone bar labels (600 on the current tab). Tracking 0, leading 1.12 to 1.2. Sizes are tokens: `--t-h1` 28 to 38px, `--t-h2` 22 to 26px, `--t-h3` 19px, `--t-name` 17px, `--t-btn` 17px. Never hard-code a heading size in a page.
     - **Lexend** 400/500/600 for everything that is read or counted: body 16px/400, table cells and heads, product names in rows, sizes and size keys, form fields, links, the nav under the bar, the basket count, and every price and total in tabular figures (`.num`). The words beside a price ("Essentials total", "Subtotal") stay Lexend too.
     - Sentence case everywhere, buttons included. No caps labels, no eyebrows, no italics. Both faces are served from this site, never from Google Fonts (see Performance and mobile).
   - Shape: 6px corners on controls (`rounded-ctl`), 2px blue borders on fields and steppers, a 2px blue rule on top of a table and 1px lines between rows. No tiles, no cards, no shadows, no gradients, no pills, no arrows on links, no middle-dot strings.
6. Tap targets ≥ 44px; no horizontal overflow at 375px; body text 16px (14px only for table heads, meta lines and the legal line).
7. **Schools are the core idea, and the header is the school search** (`SchoolSearch` in `src/components/SchoolFinder.tsx`): a solid blue bar with the lockup, the field and the basket. Do not put category links in the bar and do not add a second way to search. `SchoolTable` (Home, /schools, 404) is the same list written out: mark, name, area, "N items online" or "In store only". Under the bar, one white line names the chosen school and links to "Change school"; the choice is saved on the device (`src/lib/school.tsx`, key `iana-school`) and is made by picking a school or opening its page.
   - A school's mark (`SchoolMark`) is a real photo of one of its crested garments where one is online, otherwise its initials on the school colour; schools that are in store only get an outlined mark.
   - A school page: the essentials (priced, photographed crested items; sizes every item shares; one size chosen once; rows with one total and one button; bundle logic in `SchoolPage.tsx` is unchanged), then "From the school's uniform list" in the two groups the data supports: "With the school crest" and "Plain items to go with it". Never label items compulsory or optional until the school's leaflet is in the data. Schools with fewer than three products online (`ONLINE_MIN`) keep their short in-store page.
8. **The product unit is a row** (`ProductRow`): picture, name, size range, price, Add. "Add" opens the size and quantity table under the row; an item with one size or none is added with one tap. There is no product card and no grid: do not bring one back. Listings (`Collection.tsx`) are grouped by garment type with sticky group heads and a jump-list; `/collections/plain` is every item without a crest.
9. **The product page is the size and quantity table** (`OrderTable`): one line per size with a stepper, a "Size guide" link in the table head, then one yellow button that says how many and how much ("Add 3 to basket, £29.97"; "from" when prices vary by size, because the data has only a minimum and maximum). The button stays enabled with nothing chosen and asks for a quantity. The total and button stick to the bottom of the screen while the table is in view. The fitting line ("bring your child to the shop and we measure them: no appointment needed") sits under the table.
10. **Data hygiene lives in `tidyProduct()`** (`src/lib/shopify.ts`), used by both modes. `sizeMap` keeps the store's own option value for checkout; never send a display label to Shopify.
11. **Delivery and returns** are confirmed facts in `SITE.delivery` / `SITE.returns`; show them from there: one sentence on Home, the rows on every product page, the side column of a school page, the basket, and `/delivery`. Never hard-code the figures in a page. There is no header strip.
12. **Phones:** a fixed bottom bar with Schools, Plain basics and Basket (with the count) (`TabBar`). The header is not sticky on phones. Sticky totals sit above the bar (`--tabbar`) and the footer pads itself so its last line clears it.
13. **Footer:** white, a 2px blue rule, three lines (the shop, the links, the legal line with the imagery disclosure). No columns, no figures, no lockup.
14. **Voice:** instructions. "Pick the school." "Choose one size for all three, then add them together." No slogans, no paired-phrase headings, no benefit lines repeated across pages.
15. CSS: element and component rules live in `@layer base` / `@layer components` in `index.css` so utilities win. Motion answers an action (the school list opening, the basket, a row's sizes, changing page with `VLink`); there is no unprompted motion. Reduced motion switches all of it off. Shared elements: the school's mark (`schoolVT`) and a product's picture (`productVT`), one name per page.

## Performance and mobile (v6)
The designs do not change in this section; it records how the site stays fast and steady on a phone.
- **Fonts are self-hosted.** `@fontsource-variable/fredoka` and `@fontsource-variable/lexend`, latin subset only, one variable file each, declared by hand in `index.css` as "Fredoka" (500 to 600) and "Lexend" (400 to 600) with `font-display: swap`. `vite.config.ts` (`preloadFonts`) adds a `<link rel="preload">` for both files. Each face has a stand-in (`"Fredoka Fallback"`, `"Lexend Fallback"`: a local Arial or Roboto with `size-adjust` and ascent/descent overrides) so text does not move when the face arrives. No Google Fonts link; if a weight is added, widen the `font-weight` range, do not add a file.
- **Pictures are cut to size at build time.** `scripts/images.mjs` (sharp; runs before `vite build` and before `vite`) writes `public/img/products/w/<name>-<128|192|320|640>.webp`; the folder is ignored by git. Components never print a photo's own path: use `smallImg(src, box)` for a row picture, a school's mark or a gallery key and `largeImg(src)` for the product page picture (`src/lib/img.ts`: `srcset`, `sizes`, `width`, `height`, `decoding="async"`; Shopify photos get the CDN's `width` parameter). Everything is `loading="lazy"` except the product page picture (`fetchpriority="high"`) and the marks in the header and a school's title (`eager`). New widths go in both `WIDTHS` lists.
- **The first render is the finished page.** `main.tsx` waits for `preload()` (`src/lib/preload.ts`: schools and products, 2.5s at most) before React starts, and pages take their first state from `boot` / `bootProducts()`; they still ask the storefront in an effect. Do not add a page that renders "Loading" first when the data is already in `boot`. `scripts/prerender.mjs` renders every route as a first visit (storage cleared, the untouched app shell). The remembered school is read in the provider's first state; `index.html` sets `data-school` before the first paint so the phone's "your school" line keeps its 44px from the start.
- **Scrolling between pages** is `ScrollManager`: a new page starts at the top or at its `#anchor`, Back and Forward return to where the reader was (kept in `sessionStorage`). Pages do not call `window.scrollTo` themselves. `<Routes>` is keyed by the path so each address mounts its own page.
- **Sheets** (the basket, the size guide) use `useSheet` (`src/lib/sheet.ts`): the page behind is locked (`html.locked`), Back closes the sheet (it adds one history entry and removes it again when closed another way), Escape and tap outside close it, focus returns to the opener. A link inside a sheet that leaves the page uses `replace`. Scrolling parts take `.sheet-scroll`; the dimmed layer takes `.backdrop`.
- **Safe areas.** `.wrap` side padding is `max(1rem, env(safe-area-inset-*))`; the bottom bar, the basket and the size guide pad themselves by the insets. Anything sized to the screen uses `svh`/`dvh`, never `vh`, on phones.
- **A phone on its side** (`orientation: landscape` and `max-height: 480px`): the bottom bar is 46px, group heads are not sticky and the sticky total and button share one line (`.order-foot[data-kit]`), so fixed and sticky bars stay under a third of the height. The bottom bar slides away while the school field has the keyboard.
- **Touch.** Hover styles live inside `@media (hover: hover)`; every control has an `:active` state with no transition; `touch-action: manipulation` is set on controls and the tap highlight is off. A link in a line of text that deserves 44px (a phone number, a product's name in a row) takes `.tap-pad` or the `.row-link` padding: the box grows, the line does not. On a phone the product page reads name and price, picture (capped at `36svh`), then the table, so the name is always on the first screen; from 768px the picture stands to the left.
- **Text size.** `body` is `1rem`; do not set type in px.
- `vite preview` serves the prerendered page for `/schools/x` (plugin `previewPrerendered`), as GitHub Pages does. Checks used for this pass are in `/home/claude/builds/v6/iana-schoolwear/` (`journey.cjs`, `shots.cjs`, `lh.sh`) and `/home/claude/builds/v6/tools/sweep.cjs`.

## SEO and sharing (v7)
Every link must work and share fully on GitHub Pages (`https://sheedosa.github.io/iana-schoolwear/`). Checked with `/home/claude/builds/v7/seo/_scripts/` (`audit.mjs`, `pages-test.mjs`, `share.mjs`, `lh.sh`); results in `/home/claude/builds/v7/seo/iana-schoolwear/`.
- **Addresses end in a slash.** Pages serves `/route/` and 301s `/route` to it, so canonicals, `og:url`, breadcrumbs, the sitemap and `llms.txt` use `pageUrl(path)` from `Seo.tsx`. The sitemap lists every route in `scripts/prerender.mjs` and nothing else. `/brief/` (the client intake form) stays `noindex, nofollow` and out of the sitemap.
- **Not indexed yet (v8).** `INDEXABLE` in `src/lib/indexing.ts` (here, not in `SITE`, because `site.ts` is protected) is `false` while the facts await the client: every prerendered page carries one `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` from `Seo.tsx`, and everything else (titles, descriptions, canonicals, Open Graph, Twitter, JSON-LD, sitemap) stays. The 404 page keeps `noindex,nofollow` and `/brief/` its own tag. `robots.txt` allows everyone and must not gain a `Disallow`: link-preview bots obey it. **Flip `INDEXABLE` to `true` when the client confirms the facts and wants the site in search**, rebuild, and check `dist/` carries `index,follow`.
- **Every page's `<Seo>` gives, in the prerendered HTML:** a unique `<title>` under 60 characters, page first and "I&A Schoolwear" last; a unique description of 120 to 160 characters built with `fit()` from `SITE` and the catalogue (never cut mid-word; a plain item is never called embroidered); one canonical; the full Open Graph set (`og:type`, `og:site_name`, `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:url`, absolute `og:image`, 1200x630, `og:image:alt`, `og:locale` en_GB) and Twitter `summary_large_image`. The 404 page is `noindex` with no canonical.
- **The card.** `public/img/og/og-home.jpg` (the blue bar, lockup and "Find your school's uniform"). A photographed product has its own card in the same arrangement with the shop's own photograph as a cut-out (`public/img/og/p/<handle>.jpg`), and a school with its page online has one with its first crested item (`public/img/og/s/<slug>.jpg`): `_scripts/cards.mjs` with `cards-iana-schoolwear.mjs`, from a served `dist`. Re-run it when products, photos or `photo-rules.ts` change; it picks each picture with `firstPhoto()`, so a card never shows a hidden photo, and a product with no photo to show has no card and uses the home card. Never point `og:image` at a product photograph directly. No children, no scenes. Cards stay under 300 KB.
- **Structured data allowed:** `ClothingStore` (address, telephone, hours, memberships from `SITE`) on every page, `WebSite` with the school search on Home, `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages, `Product` with name, description, image, brand, SKU and `offers` from the catalogue's own price (prices are the store's); **no `offers` when the price is 0** ("Price in store") and **no `availability`**: we make no stock claims. No placeholder or unconfirmed fact in JSON-LD.
- **Head:** `lang="en-GB"`, theme colour School Blue `#4169e1` (the bar), `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png` and the SVG marque (`img/logo/brand/iana-marque-blue.svg`), 180px `apple-touch-icon`, `site.webmanifest` with `scope`, 192, 512 and a maskable 512 icon. Every path carries `/iana-schoolwear/`.
- **Deep links:** `public/404.html` (noindex) sends an unknown path to `/?p=`, `main.tsx` restores it, and a path no route matches shows the not-found page. `public/.nojekyll` is deployed. Search engines read only `https://sheedosa.github.io/robots.txt` for a project site.

## The hub demo (`public/hub/`)
`public/hub/` is not part of the shop: it is the built demo of the I&A Hub dashboard (source in the private `iana-platform` repo, built with `HUB_BASE=/iana-schoolwear/hub npm run build:demo` and copied here), parked on this site only so it has a link to open on a phone. It is `noindex`, uses demo data only, is not linked from any page and is not in the sitemap. Do not edit it by hand; delete the folder when the hub has its own host.
